Where to start · everyday work

Less time in the inbox, with tools you already have

Most people start at . They do not need agents yet. They need help with e-mails and paperwork, in German and in Microsoft 365 and Copilot, which you already use. Five everyday tasks with example prompts:

Summarise a long e-mail thread

Fasse diesen E-Mail-Verlauf in fünf Punkten zusammen. Was wird von mir erwartet, und bis wann?

Five points, what is expected of me and by when.

Write a reply in the right tone

Schreib eine kurze, sachliche Antwort an den Lieferanten in der Sie-Form: Termin am Donnerstag bestätigen, aktuelle Prüfprotokolle anfordern.

Short and factual, formal address: confirm Thursday, ask for the latest test reports.

Turn meeting notes into tasks

Mach aus diesen Besprechungsnotizen eine Aufgabenliste: was, wer, bis wann. Offene Fragen bitte extra.

What, who, by when, with open questions listed separately.

Translate or simplify a technical document

Übersetze dieses Datenblatt ins Englische und erkläre die drei wichtigsten Kennwerte in einfachen Worten.

Translate the data sheet and explain the three key values in plain words.

Ask questions about a long PDF

Wo steht in dieser Wartungsanleitung, wie oft das Sieb gewechselt werden muss? Bitte die Stelle mit Seitenzahl zitieren.

Find the passage in the maintenance manual and quote it with the page number.

How we teach it

Short hands-on sessions by level, on people's own e-mails and documents: in German for teams outside IT, in English for IT. Prompt cards for the desk and an AI ambassador in each team. We have run introductory workshops like this for non-IT teams, including a legal department, and Level 1 training for 270+ people at Česká spořitelna.

We would start here because people see the effect on their own work quickly and stay in control of it. And the tools are already paid for.

Before

Technical inquiries were handled by hand and took several days each.

What we did

An AI assistant splits an inquiry from e-mail, PDF, scan or photo (three languages) into items and fills 30+ parameters. Confidence is calculated outside the model from verified citations. An expert approves every item; corrections become rules.

Result

A prefilled form in a few minutes. 99,1 % of 3 432 values correct on 50 real inquiries. 231 automated tests on real documents.
